# Glyphs

`home.svg`, `settings.svg` and `library.svg` (Phosphor's "books") are from
[Phosphor Icons](https://phosphoricons.com), Light weight, under the MIT license. `keyboard.svg`, `mouse.svg` and `mark.svg` are
Ectomatter's own (the mouse drawn in the keyboard's style), as is the lab's flask, drawn as a path in `app.slint`.

`mark.svg` is Ectomatter's mark at the size the title bar shows it, 16 pixels: the family splat with a ring. It and
the icons in `../icons` (the tray's and the desktop's, one file per size) are made by `tools/mark/build_icons.py`;
change the drawing there, not here.
