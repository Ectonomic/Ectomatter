Weather, once, for every device

The M3's weather feed moves into Ectodeck itself. One place, chosen once in Settings, and every device reads the same report: the M3's weather backgrounds, the Z10's weather effect and weather data on its screen, and any device added later. Search below: it asks Open-Meteo for real, the same service the M3 uses.

Every device shows the same sky. Change the place here and they all follow.
Weather and air from Open-Meteo (CC BY 4.0), every 15 minutes. Places from Open-Meteo, and from Photon over OpenStreetMap (ODbL) for names Open-Meteo cannot match. Warnings every 3 minutes from the National Weather Service, Environment and Climate Change Canada, or MeteoAlarm (CC BY 4.0).
  • Where it sits: Settings, under Devices. It is Ectodeck's, not one device's, so it belongs to the app level.
  • Units default from the computer's region (Fahrenheit and mph in the United States, Celsius with mph in the United Kingdom, metres a second in the Nordic countries), and are chosen once for every device. The M3's shaders read the raw report, so units only change what the Z10 writes.
  • Search uses the computer's language: a Russian computer finds Москва, a Japanese one finds 東京都, and each gets its Latin name for the screen.
  • Offline, the last report stands, as the M3's feed already does, and the Now line says how old it is.
  • Already built: the shared feed (ported from the M3's weather module, its tests passing, plus a place search) runs inside Ectodeck now, and reads the place from Settings. This screen is the only missing part.
