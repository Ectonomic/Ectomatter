Ectodeck home

A minimal rail down the left, a shade of its own: Home, then one glyph for each device, named when you point at it. Clicking the item you are already on opens it into a blade with each device's name, animated, and the page makes room. The home is the devices' glyphs and names, nothing else.

01 Home

Only the devices: each one's glyph for its kind, in the same plain grey square, and its name. You pass through it on the way to a device.

—☐✕
  • Every device shows its kind's glyph, from Ectodeck's own set for desk peripherals (section 03). Product pictures are kept for inside the device, where the editor works on a drawing of it.
  • The only other thing a tile ever says is "Not connected", under the name of a device that is unplugged, which dims. The last tile shows one, as an example. Everything else, firmware and profiles included, is inside the device.
  • The rail never carries status. Closed, a device's name appears when you point at it (shown on the MagTran); open, it shows the names. The place you are in is lighter, and an unplugged device dims. Click an item to go there; click the item you are already on to open the blade, and again to close it, as VS Code's activity bar does. Nothing on screen is spent on it (section 02 shows the rail open).
  • Opened, the blade takes its room from the page, which narrows to make space. Whether it was left open is remembered.
  • Settings sits at the rail's foot, with cores and devices inside it, and nothing sits under it; the title bar is left with only Ectodeck and the window's controls.

02 Inside a device

The Z10 chosen in the rail, with the rail opened into its blade: each device's name, and nothing more. Click the Z10, the item you are on, to close it and again to open it; try it in either window. This is the real editor, reading your keyboard.

03 The artwork system

The same keyboard at each level, so the fall-back is plain. A core supplies what it can; Ectodeck shows the best of it and never needs more to work.

ViewStraight down from above, the view the editors work in. The device only, on a transparent ground, with no shadow or backdrop of its own: Ectodeck frames every device the same way.
UnlitKeys and screens drawn unlit, so the same picture can be shown dark on the home and in its real colours inside the device.
Fitted to a squareEctodeck fits every picture inside a square, centred, keeping its shape. Artwork is cropped close to the device, with no empty margin of its own.
Format and licenceSVG, or PNG or WebP at twice the size. Its licence is declared in the core's manifest and must allow the picture to be shared, which a maker's product photo usually does not.
