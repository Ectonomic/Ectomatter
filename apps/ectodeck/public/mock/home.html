Ectodeck home

What Ectodeck opens on, now that it is a platform. It follows the pattern Logitech G Hub and SteelSeries GG settled on: your devices, each drawn as the real thing, and a click takes you into one. The Z10 below is the real keyboard view, reading your keyboard; the M3 shows a frame from its own renderer.

01 Home

Every connected device, drawn live: the Z10 lit as it is right now with its display running, the M3 showing what its screen shows. Under each, what you need to know at a glance, and the way in.

  • Each card is as tall as its device, so the keyboard and the deck sit on one baseline, the way they sit on the desk, and neither floats in space.
