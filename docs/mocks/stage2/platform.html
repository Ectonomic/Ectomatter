Ectodeck

Platform mock · one app for every device on the desk

Ectodeck keeps the window it has today: a title bar that reads as a path (Ectodeck, then the device, then the profile), the device drawn on its stage, a library underneath of what you can put on it, and an inspector on the right for whatever is selected. What changes is that the device can be anything a core describes. The keyboard below is the real editor, reading your Z10; the M3's screen is frames from its own renderer.

01 EVGA Z10 RGB · Keys

The Z10's keys, with E2 chosen. What E2 does moves into the inspector, where Ectodeck already shows a key's action; the library below offers everything a key can be given, with your macros first.

  • The keyboard stores its own profiles, so its screens carry Discard and Save to keyboard, at the stage's right, only while there are changes; the profile in the title bar shows a dot until they are saved.
  • Keys, Lighting, Display and Settings are the Z10 core's capabilities. Each device shows only the ones it has.
  • Macro Editor sits in the title bar because macros belong to the platform, not to one device.

02 EVGA Z10 RGB · Lighting

Lighting on the same frame: the effects are the library, and the chosen effect's settings fill the inspector, drawn from the core's description in the shared settings language.

03 VSDinside MagTran M3 · Keys

The M3 as Ectodeck shows it today, now as one device among others. The screen is the live feed from the deck core: what the deck shows, the app shows.

04 VSDinside MagTran M3 · Screen

The backgrounds are the deck core's catalogue, with thumbnails. Choosing one plays a preview on the stage without touching the deck; Apply sends it.

05 Switching devices

The device in the title bar opens a list of every connected device, from every core. Cores are managed in Settings.
