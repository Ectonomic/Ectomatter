Ectotype

Stage 2 mock · the desktop app in the Ectonomic family's look

These are the real editor screens, reading your keyboard, restyled and set into the app's windows. Nothing here writes to the keyboard or shows a preview on it. Everything you approved stays as it was, except the changes listed under each window.

01 The two windows

Ectotype opens in its main window, which holds Keys, Lighting and Settings. The Macro Editor is its own window, opened from the title bar or from a key that plays a macro. Both are drawn the way Ectodeck draws its window: rounded corners, a hairline edge, and a title bar of their own.

Main window, on the Keys tab with E2 chosen
Macro Editor window
  • The title bar carries the mark and the wordmark, then the three tabs, then the keyboard's status and the Macro Editor button, then the window's own controls.
  • The interface is set in Geist, the family's face. The keycap legends keep their condensed face, which copies EVGA's printing on the real keys.
  • The greys are the family's zinc neutrals on a black ground. Lime appears only as a signal: focus, the connection and live dots, and the box around a key you have changed.
  • On a key that plays a macro, "Edit macro" opens it in the Macro Editor window instead of scrolling down.

02 In the tray

Ectotype keeps running in the tray when its windows are closed, so the display keeps showing your stats and the keys keep their assignments. The menu is drawn by the system, so on KDE it looks like this, and on Windows and macOS like theirs.

03 The mark

Every product in the family is the same lime blob with one simple glyph in dark lime. Ectodeck already has a grid of keys, so Ectotype needs a different one. The keyboard, with its spacebar, still reads as a keyboard at tray and title-bar sizes; the key with a cursor shrinks to a square with a bar, which reads as a pause button.
