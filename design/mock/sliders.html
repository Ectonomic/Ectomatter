The Z10's two sliders, as controls you choose

The keyboard has a dimmer and a volume slider. Both are real potentiometers, so the keyboard always knows exactly where each one is; until now it kept that to itself. With Ectomatter's firmware it says, and the app can put each slider on whatever you want it to control. This sits under the keyboard on the Settings page.

How they behave

On what the keyboard does by itself, the dimmer sets the backlight and the volume slider sends volume keys. Put either on something else and Ectomatter drives it from the slider's own position, so it lands exactly where you put it.

What each slider controls is Ectomatter's, not the keyboard's, so it is the same across all five profiles and applies while Ectomatter is running.

  • The reading is the point. The keyboard knows both sliders to about one part in four thousand. Showing that, live, is the first thing in Ectomatter that no other software for this keyboard can do.
  • Absolute, not stepped. Put the volume slider on system volume through Ectomatter and it sets the volume to where the slider is, rather than sending a string of volume-up presses. Nothing is lost on a fast sweep, and the bottom of the travel stops swallowing presses to leave mute.

03 The volume slider on something else

The keyboard sends volume keys from that slider on its own, underneath anything Ectomatter does with it. Moving it would change the volume as well as the thing you chose, so Ectomatter stops those keys reaching the computer while it is running.

EVGA's firmware keeps both sliders to itself: the dimmer sets the backlight, the volume slider sends volume keys, and neither can be read or put on anything else. Ectomatter's firmware adds both, and the Custom firmware panel below installs it.

05 What this needs

  • Reading both sliders is in the firmware already, flashed today.
  • Setting the volume, the microphone or an app is the computer's own audio, which Ectomatter can set exactly.
  • Holding the backlight while the dimmer is on something else is in the firmware already.
  • Holding back the volume slider's own keys is the one piece still to build. Ectomatter can do it on the computer, because it can see the slider move and knows the keys are the slider's; a later firmware could stop them at the keyboard instead, which is tidier but needs another flash. My preference is the first now and the second later, so nothing waits on firmware.
