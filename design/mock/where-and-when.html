Every control has its four stepsRest, hover, chosen, chosen and hovered, from the app's ladder: the switch and chips sit on the well (#131316), lighten to #24242A, are chosen at #36363D and #3E3E46. No borders, no white fills.
The keyboard is the mapOn Some areas, an area on the picture lights up under the pointer and a click turns it on or off, the same as its chip. On Whole keyboard the picture isn't clickable; a chip there still shows where its area is.
Nothing movesPlays stays where it is whichever way Where is set. On Whole keyboard the areas stay in view, faded, and picking one switches to Some areas with just that area.
One choice for whenThe keyboard stores a start and a stop. Plays names the six pairs as what you'd see, so the summary sentence is gone: the choice is the sentence.

What Plays stores

Each choice is one start and stop pair the firmware already has (EVGA's LedStartMode and LedStopMode). Nothing new is sent to the keyboard.

Why it's close. It shows the keyboard's own two settings as they are, and it fits the box too.

Why it lost. You still build the outcome from two parts, and a pair like "starts with the profile, stops on key release" reads as a sentence while meaning little. Plays gives you the six outcomes by name.

Also tried and dropped: picking areas only on the picture, with no chips. The keyboard is a good map, but nothing in the box would tell you it can be clicked.
