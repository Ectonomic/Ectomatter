# Ectodeck's typefaces for legends

The faces keyboards are printed in, built into Ectodeck so a setup's legends look the same on any computer. Each is under a license that lets Ectodeck carry it: the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`OFL.txt`, with each font's own copyright in the font) or MIT (`OpenGorton-LICENSE.txt`). Each is cut to the letters keyboards print, a variable font keeping all its weights; Audiowide and Orbitron, whose licenses reserve their names, are carried as they come.

- `BrunoAce-Regular.ttf`: wide and technical. https://github.com/googlefonts/Bruno-ace, OFL-1.1.
- `Audiowide-Regular.ttf`: rounded and technical, a slashed zero. https://github.com/googlefonts/audiowide, OFL-1.1, Reserved Font Name "Audiowide".
- `Orbitron.ttf`: geometric and square, the EVGA Z10's own look. https://github.com/theleagueof/orbitron, OFL-1.1, Reserved Font Name "Orbitron".
- `Michroma-Regular.ttf`: wide and squared, the wide Eurostile look. https://github.com/googlefonts/Michroma-font, OFL-1.1.
- `Saira.ttf`: squared, the Eurostile look of gaming keyboards. https://github.com/Omnibus-Type/Saira, OFL-1.1.
- `Rajdhani-Medium.ttf`: narrow and squared. https://github.com/itfoundry/rajdhani, OFL-1.1.
- `Rajdhani-SemiBold.ttf`: narrow and squared. https://github.com/itfoundry/rajdhani, OFL-1.1.
- `Rajdhani-Bold.ttf`: narrow and squared. https://github.com/itfoundry/rajdhani, OFL-1.1.
- `Barlow-Regular.ttf`: plain and upright, the DIN look. https://github.com/jpt/barlow, OFL-1.1.
- `Barlow-SemiBold.ttf`: plain and upright, the DIN look. https://github.com/jpt/barlow, OFL-1.1.
- `Arimo.ttf`: the Helvetica and Arial look most keyboards print. https://github.com/googlefonts/arimo, OFL-1.1.
- `Inter.ttf`: the clean modern look of laptop keyboards. https://github.com/rsms/inter, OFL-1.1.
- `Nunito.ttf`: soft and rounded, the VAG Rounded look of older Mac keyboards. https://github.com/googlefonts/nunito, OFL-1.1.
- `Jost.ttf`: round and geometric, the Futura look. https://github.com/indestructible-type/Jost, OFL-1.1.
- `OpenGorton-Regular.otf`: the engraved Gorton of classic keyboards. https://github.com/dakotafelder/open-gorton, MIT.
- `OpenGorton-Bold.otf`: the engraved Gorton of classic keyboards. https://github.com/dakotafelder/open-gorton, MIT.
- `JetBrainsMono.ttf`: a programmer's monospace. https://github.com/JetBrains/JetBrainsMono, OFL-1.1.
- `CourierPrime-Regular.ttf`: the typewriter look. https://github.com/quoteunquoteapps/CourierPrime, OFL-1.1.
- `CourierPrime-Bold.ttf`: the typewriter look. https://github.com/quoteunquoteapps/CourierPrime, OFL-1.1.
- `NotoSansJP.ttf`: Japanese keyboards' kana and keys. https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk, OFL-1.1.
- `NotoSansKR.ttf`: Korean keyboards' jamo and keys. https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk, OFL-1.1.

Built by `tools/typefaces.py`.
